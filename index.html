A little farm, a very big search

FIND THE NEEDLE
IN THE HAYSTACK

Somewhere in this pile... there's a needle.

Start with your hands, sell hay at the Hay Shop, and upgrade your tools and carrying capacity at the Tool Shop. Spend your earnings to open new farm areas, discover rare hay and hidden caches, build an apiary, and make your farm your own. Progress is saved in this browser.

Find the needle while building your farm kit. Hay varieties range from common $1 pieces to valuable rarities; check your inventory value and visit the HAY SHOP to choose what to sell. Visit the TOOL SHOP for stronger rakes or a metal detector. Sell your remaining hay before finishing the search.

Rare treasures are hidden in hay: remove hay to reveal them, then press E within 2 meters to collect. Sell collected treasures only at the separate Treasure Shop; the Metal Detector searches for the needle, not treasure.

Grow pumpkins at the pumpkin patch: buy seeds at the PUMPKIN MARKET, plant them in empty beds, and harvest them when ripe. Return to the market to sell your pumpkins. Buy garden expansions to unlock six extra beds, or spend coins on lanterns and other farm decorations.

Visit the TOOL SHOP's farm expansions to unlock the woods to the east, the old barn to the west, and the windmill meadow to the north. Search each unlocked area for a cache with special hay and coins; the hay types you discover can also turn up in the haystack.

Buy the FARM APIARY from the Tool Shop. Collect honey there every minute to sell it or plant special flowers that bloom after a little while. Use the Tool Shop's farm customization section to repaint your barn or add a garden fence, stone path, bench, and farm sign.
