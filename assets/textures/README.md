# Generated farm textures

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Used in `script.js` for the ground, haystack, and shop hay bales. Original materials remain available if loading fails.

## hay.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless tileable game surface albedo texture, square, high resolution. Primary request: a realistic photographic top-down close-up of dry golden farm hay, tightly packed thin straw stalks running mostly vertically with subtle bends and broken fibers, small variations in pale beige and muted gold, occasional desaturated olive strands. Full frame material scan, orthographic view, perfectly even diffuse overcast lighting, fine fibrous surface detail, natural restrained color, no dramatic shadows, no perspective, no objects, no border, no text. Seamless repeating edges. This image will be mapped onto individual 3D hay stalks and farm hay bales; small elongated straw fibers must remain readable.

## ground.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless tileable square ground albedo texture for a realistic 3D farm game. Primary request: photographic material scan of a worn rural grassy farmyard seen directly overhead at orthographic angle. A subtle evenly mixed mosaic of short muted green grass, dry beige grass, exposed warm brown compact earth, tiny pebbles and a few scattered straw fragments. Each patch is small, no large central feature, no path or trail. Natural fine organic surface detail, restrained realistic colors. Flat diffuse overcast illumination, no cast shadows or baked directional lighting, no horizon, no perspective, no objects, no text or borders. Uniform scale throughout; seamless repeating edges.
